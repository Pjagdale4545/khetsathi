Our Mission

Empowering Farmers, Feeding India

KhetSaathi, powered by Sunsys Techsol Pvt. Ltd., is an innovative agricultural platform designed to deliver fresh crops, vegetables, and fruits directly from farmers to buyers, minimizing intermediaries and inefficiencies in the supply chain.

🔹 Our Platform Role: KhetSaathi reduces unnecessary parking, re-parling, loading, and reloading of produce between farmer locations, mandis, and buyers. By streamlining the supply chain, we enhance farmer income by cutting down Mandi brokerage and providing buyers with quality produce at slightly lower rates — creating a win-win for farmers, buyers, and society.

🔹 Field to Buyer: Farmers also have the option to sell directly from their fields, ensuring freshness, transparency, and better profits.

Guided by a social mission, KhetSaathi champions Indian farmers, supporting their livelihoods and acknowledging their invaluable role in feeding the nation.
