Our Mission

At Khetsathi (meaning "Farmer's Friend"), our main goal is to help you get the best price for your crops. We have a simple website that connects you directly with buyers. This way, buyers can see many farmers and find good produce, and you can talk to different buyers to get the best deal.

We believe that when farmers do well, everyone in the village and country does well. We are here to support you every step of the way.
